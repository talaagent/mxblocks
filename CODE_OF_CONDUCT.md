# Code of conduct

Everyone participating in mxblocks discussions, issues, pull requests, and other
project spaces should be able to contribute without harassment.

Treat people with respect. Discuss the code and evidence, explain disagreements,
and accept corrections. Be considerate of contributors with different experience,
backgrounds, identities, and communication styles.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory remarks, sexualized behavior, repeated
unwanted contact, and publishing another person's private information are not
acceptable. Do not include private messages, credentials, or identifying data in
public reproductions.

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainer at [github@igm.pub](mailto:github@igm.pub).
Include the relevant links and context. Reports will be handled as confidentially
as possible, and reporters should not face retaliation.

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content, limit participation, or block
participants when needed. They will consider the context and severity of an
incident, explain decisions to the people involved when appropriate, and apply
these expectations to themselves as well as other contributors.
